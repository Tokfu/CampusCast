    About & API

    What is CampusCast?

    A student-centered weather app. Instead of only showing raw numbers, it turns the forecast into school-day advice: umbrella, water, clothing, and whether outdoor activities are a good idea.

    Both APIs are public, so the app needs no key or sign-in – the full access-method details are in the table below.
